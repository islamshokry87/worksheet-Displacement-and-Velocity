Answer Key — Grade 9 Physics · Section 2.1 Displacement and Velocity

  1. A — frame of reference (1 mark)
  2. B — $\Delta x = x_{\text{f}} - x_{\text{i}}$ (1 mark)
  3. C — Velocity (1 mark)
  4. m/s (or meters per second) (1 mark)
  5. velocity (or average velocity) (1 mark)
  6. 61 cm — Solution: $\Delta x = x_{\text{f}} - x_{\text{i}} = 85\text{ cm} - 24\text{ cm} = +61\text{ cm}$. (1 mark)
  7. 825 m (or $825\text{ m east}$) — Solution: $\Delta x = v_{\text{avg}} \cdot \Delta t = (6.02\text{ m/s})(137\text{ s}) = 824.74\text{ m} \approx 825\text{ m}$. (1 mark)
  8. 3 hours (or $3\text{ h}$) — Solution: $\Delta t = \frac{\Delta x}{v_{\text{avg}}} = \frac{144\text{ km}}{48.0\text{ km/h}} = 3.0\text{ h}$. (1 mark)
  9. 3.5 hours (or $3.5\text{ h}$) — Solution: $\Delta t = \frac{\Delta x}{v_{\text{avg}}} = \frac{280\text{ km}}{80\text{ km/h}} = 3.5\text{ h}$. (1 mark)
  10. C — At rest (zero velocity) (1 mark)
  11. B — Slope of the tangent line at that point (1 mark)
  12. a) Object 3   b) Object 2   c) Object 1 (3 marks total, 1 mark each)
  13. Both undergo the exact same displacement.
    Reasoning: Displacement depends only on the initial position (Florida) and final position (California), $\Delta x = x_{\text{f}} - x_{\text{i}}$. Even though the astronauts traveled a much greater total distance orbiting Earth, their net change in position is identical to that of the photographer. (2 marks)
